Booth's poverty map today

Charles Booth's colours of 1898 to 1899 read along every street of today's inner London, against deprivation in 2025. Slide between then and now; tap a street or an area.

%%BYLINE%%

    Layers

    Coverage

    Booth's 1898 to 1899 map covers inner London only: from Hammersmith to Greenwich, Hampstead to Brixton. Streets are coloured only where they lie on the map and the colour could be read. The thin brown outline marks the %%NLSOA%% areas analysed.
